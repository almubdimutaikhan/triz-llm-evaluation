# Research artifact constraints

- Rebuild numerical results with `python scripts/analyze.py`; render figures from that JSON with `python scripts/figures.py`.
- Use only cross-family, order-consistent votes for headline preference estimates. Collapse both orders to one vote and bootstrap whole cases.
- Treat DeepSeek aliases as the same family. Do not inflate independent case counts with votes, models, rejudgements, or reused datasets.
- The factorial folder was called `factorial_129`; only 40 cases are completed in this snapshot.
- No clear TRIZ advantage under structured controls is established. Wide intervals are not equivalence evidence.
- Selection results are a one-problem case study with overlapping slates. Reliability is not correctness or proven agent-task improvement.
- Preserve evidence hashes and provenance; reimport explicitly when changing snapshots. Do not silently overwrite historical evidence.
- Do not publish credentials, correspondence, original problem/model text, or third-party prompt descriptions. MIT applies to newly authored code only.
- Research direction is proposed work. Do not imply faculty endorsement, supervision, or publication acceptance.
- Run the regression checks and visually inspect changed figures before release.
